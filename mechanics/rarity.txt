Mortal → Cursed → Arcane → Forbidden → Mythic → Relic → Divine
Based on the backgrounds and descriptions provided, here is a logical rarity classification for the first 100 cards.

To ensure consistency, the rarities were assigned using the following criteria based on the lore:

 Mortal: Standard human soldiers, mercenaries, rangers, rogues, and non-magical royals.


 Cursed: Undead troops, ghouls, lycanthropes, trolls, and lesser corrupted beasts.


 Arcane: Spellcasters, paladins channeling celestial energy, dryads, and magical spirits.


 Forbidden: Dark cultists, vampire lords, death-knight warlords, and nightmarish entities.


 Mythic: Colossal dragons, elemental titans, frost giants, and ethereal leviathans.


 Relic: Ancient, sentient artifacts of immense power.


 Divine: Primordial terrors and god-like entities capable of shattering seals or reality.
