Abyss

Mechanic: All card abilities are disabled (silenced). The Power of every card at this location is divided by 2 (halved).

Battlefield

Mechanic: Continuous buff. Every card at this location gains bonus Power equal to the total number of cards currently placed at this location (combining the card counts of both players).

Coliseum

Trigger: End of round.

Mechanic: Evaluate the winner of this location. The winning player's single highest-Power card at this location permanently doubles its current Power and doubles the numerical effects of its abilities.

Cursed Lands

Trigger: End of each turn.

Mechanic: Every card at this location receives -1 Power.

Dense Forest

Mechanic: Vision restriction. Each player can only see exactly 50% of the opponent's cards placed at this location. The remaining cards are hidden from the opponent's UI.

Desert

Mechanic: Capacity restriction. A maximum of 4 cards can be played at this location per player (standard board slot limit).

Dragons

Trigger: When a card is played here.

Mechanic: The card undergoes an RNG check. There is a 30% probability the card is immediately destroyed ("burned").

Elaria

Mechanic: Faction buff. Any card possessing the "Elarian" faction tag has its Power multiplied by 2.

Elderlands

Mechanic: Faction buff. Any card possessing the "Elderveld" faction tag has its Power multiplied by 2.

Future

Trigger: Scoring phase / Start of next round.

Mechanic: Cards placed here contribute 0 Power to the current round's score. At the start of the next round, all cards at this location automatically move to the new active location.

Hochreich

Mechanic: Faction buff. Any card possessing the "Hochreich" faction tag has its Power multiplied by 2.

HotC Camp

Mechanic: Faction buff. Any card possessing the "Horde of the Carrion" faction tag has its Power multiplied by 2.

Kal

Mechanic: Faction debuff. Any card that does not possess the "Kalthrone" faction tag has its Power divided by 2 (halved).

Mirror

Trigger: When a card is played here.

Mechanic: The card undergoes an RNG check. There is a 50% probability that the card changes ownership and is placed on the opponent's side of the location instead.

Hell

Mechanic: Play restriction. Only cards possessing the "Morthal", "Cursed", or "Arcane" tags can be played at this location. All other cards are blocked.

Intestines

Mechanic: Stat override. The Power of every card at this location is strictly set to 1, overriding all base stats, buffs, or debuffs.

Labyrinth

Trigger: When a card is played here.

Mechanic: The card undergoes an RNG check. There is a 50% probability the card is permanently transformed into the card "Blobeye" (Card ID: 0214).

Lava

Trigger: State check (Continuous).

Mechanic: If the total combined Power of all cards at this location (summing both players' scores) exceeds exactly 20, the single highest-Power card at this location is immediately destroyed.

Plague

Trigger: End of each turn.

Mechanic: Every card at this location receives -1 Power. If any card's Power reaches exactly 0, it is immediately removed from the board.

Heaven

Mechanic: Protection aura. Cards at this location are immune to destruction effects and immune to debuffs caused by opponent abilities.

Plains

Mechanic: Neutral zone. This location has no special rules, buffs, nerfs, or environmental hazards.

Ravaria

Mechanic: Faction buff. Any card possessing the "Ravarian" faction tag has its Power multiplied by 2.

Random

Trigger: Start of the round.

Mechanic: 8 random cards from each player's deck are automatically extracted and played at this location at no energy/turn cost to the players.

Shadowland

Mechanic: Vision restriction. The card identities, abilities, and Power values of all opponent cards at this location are completely hidden (face-down) until the round resolves.

Stakes

Trigger: End of round.

Mechanic: The player who wins this location receives one "Divine Dragon" card, which is added to their hand for use in the next round.

Valhalla

Mechanic: Resurrection. Cards at this location cannot be permanently destroyed or "burned." If a destruction effect triggers on a card here, the card immediately resurrects and returns to its slot on the board.

Varkaz

Mechanic: Play restriction. Cards that possess text abilities cannot be played here. Only "Vanilla" (no-ability) cards are permitted.

Snowy

Mechanic: Neutral zone. This location has no special rules, buffs, nerfs, or environmental hazards.

Soulland

Mechanic 1: Ability buff. Cards that possess text abilities gain +3 Power.

Mechanic 2: RNG Transformation. Cards with no abilities have a 10% probability to permanently transform into a "Monster" card upon being played.

Swamps

Trigger: End of round.

Mechanic: The player with the lower score at this location (the loser) automatically receives three "Werewolf" cards. Randomly selected these cards (0046, 0064, 0072, 0076, 0087, 0112)
